Trust & Data Integrity

The Zero-Hallucination Safeguard

Ambiguity Detection Pattern

The "3000 Diye" Conundrum

A shopkeeper records: "3000 diye."
Did they pay a vendor? Give cash to their family? Pay shop rent? Receive customer debt?

❌ Generic AI: Guesses "Expense: PKR 3,000" (Corrupts data)
✔️ Karobar Saathi: Flags entry_type: "unclear"
Conversational Clarification Prompt:

"Bikri ki amount kya hai ya ye paise kisko diye the?"

Fintech Engine

Converting Voice Notes into Underwriting Evidence

Microfinance banks don't have time to read thousands of ledger lines. We distill raw records into statistical credit signals.

1. Transaction Consistency Index Active Days / 30

Proves operational stability. A shopkeeper recording 26 out of 30 days demonstrates high discipline and continuous trade.

2. Sales Volatility (Coefficient of Variation) CV < 20% = Low Risk

Evaluates week-over-week revenue variance to detect whether income is steady or subject to wild seasonal swings.

3. Net Cash Buffer Runway Net Cash / Daily Expenses

Calculates how many days of operating expenses are covered if revenue momentarily halts. Vital for loan repayment safety.

Privacy by Design

Shopkeeper Sovereignty: Consent-Gated Architecture

We refuse predatory data harvesting. The shopkeeper retains absolute legal and technical ownership of their financial records.

🔐

Cryptographic Consent Gate

The Evidence API enforces mandatory X-User-Consent: true validation combined with database-level consent flags.

⚡

One-Tap Instant Revocation

If the shopkeeper flips the privacy switch off in their app, external API queries are immediately blocked with HTTP 403 Forbidden.

No Third-Party Ad Tracking

No advertising SDKs, no behavioral trackers. Financial data is never monetized or exposed to commercial data brokers.
